Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from Our Supporters

Can I co-sponsor a light with my friends or coworkers?

Yes! As we say: “Just you — or you and what army??? 💪” You can pool funds together to sponsor a $265 sign or $361 complete care set through either PayPal button above.

Why is a rechargeable battery included in the $361 set?

Pediatric transplant patients often have to walk the corridors with IV poles. The rechargeable battery pack allows their sign to be portable, illuminating their path wherever they go in the ward.

Does the child get to keep the sign when leaving?

Yes! Once treatment is completed and they ring the hospital victory bell, the neon sign travels home to hang permanently on their bedroom wall as a badge of honor.
